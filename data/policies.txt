[Refund Policy]
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

[Delivery Policy]
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. If an order is delayed beyond 10 business days, the customer is eligible for a full refund or a free replacement.

[Technical Support]
For software login issues, ask the customer to clear their browser cache or reset their password using the 'Forgot Password' link.
